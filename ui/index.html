Donate your battle replays to TFD to power community stats, contests and challenges built on data the public API can't provide.

A replay contains the battle data of all players in the match, including their names and in-game chat. Only *.wowsreplay files are uploaded — nothing else from your disk. Uploads are anonymous (no account needed) and are attributed to the in-game player name stored inside the file. Already-uploaded replays stay donated if you opt out later.
